# Chains — Product Vision (durable, rarely changes)

Chains · Fantasy DGPT is a fantasy disc golf league app (draft real pro players, score off real tournament
results) plus Go Throw, a personal round tracker, for a private group of friends (Guillermo's league). Owned and
directed by Guillermo; he is not a developer and should never need to be one to keep this running.

North star: the app should get more reliable and more complete over time with minimal manual involvement from
Guillermo beyond approving real product decisions (Tier 3) and periodically running an Engineer session with
Claude Design when there's real work queued and verified ready.

Ongoing product lanes (not exhaustive, see Issues for current state):
- Fantasy leagues, drafts, standings, and scoring
- Tournament/player-data ingestion (PDGA, course catalog)
- Live and completed-event scoring
- Mobile usability and onboarding
- League commissioner controls
- Reliability, data accuracy, backups
- Privacy, authentication, and supportability (currently minimal auth — see D-004/D-005 in decisions.md)
- Premium features / personal live-round scoring (later, not started)

Not in scope right now (Tier 3, needs its own approved plan): the future separate public multi-user app with
real Firebase email/password accounts (see decisions.md D-005).
