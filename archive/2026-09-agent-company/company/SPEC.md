# CHAINS — THE OWNER'S COMPLETE FEATURE SPECIFICATION

**This is the source of truth, in the owner's own words.** Do not paraphrase it, do not "improve" it,
do not let a summary replace it. `company/briefs/ROADMAP.md` sequences this into phases — that file is
*derived from this one*. If they ever disagree, **this file wins**.

Target quality bar, stated by the owner repeatedly: **UDisc**. The differentiator we have that nobody
else does is that the **fantasy season and the player's own game live in one app**. Every phase should
deepen that link rather than chase UDisc's feature count.

---

## 1. Course Discovery
Search a worldwide directory by: course name · city or state · ZIP code · current location · distance
from the player. Display as an interactive map or a searchable list.

Filters: distance · overall rating · difficulty · number of holes · free or paid · beginner-friendly ·
family-friendly · dog-friendly · cart-friendly · wheelchair accessible · restrooms available · scenic ·
popular · public or private · permanent or temporary.

Each listing can include: course name and location · overall rating · description · current playing
conditions · photographs · hole maps · available layouts · hole distances · pars · estimated playing
time · difficulty rating · parking information · admission or parking fees · hours of operation ·
access restrictions · contact information · amenities · written reviews · directions · layout
leaderboards · nearby stores and courses.

Players can also: add courses to a wishlist · mark courses as played · track how many they have played ·
save favorites · view recently visited · find nearby · browse top-rated · view state, national and
worldwide rankings.

## 2. Current Course Conditions
Report conditions: excellent · good · playable · muddy · wet · snow-covered · icy · flooded ·
overgrown · under construction · partially closed · temporarily unavailable.

Each update can show: date and time submitted · who submitted it · written notes · photographs ·
specific affected holes · expected duration. Course managers can post official closures, warnings and
maintenance announcements.

## 3. Course Maps and Navigation
Interactive GPS map showing: tee locations · basket locations · fairways · doglegs · walking paths ·
out-of-bounds areas · mandatory routes · drop zones · water hazards · parking · restrooms · practice
baskets · course entrances · next-tee directions.

During a round: see current position · locate tee and basket · see fairway shape · distance to basket ·
alternate tee and basket positions · select next hole · read hole instructions · navigate to next tee ·
see warnings and hazards · view a photo of the hole or tee sign.

## 4. Course Layouts
Multiple layouts per course: short · long · amateur · professional · mixed · junior · women's ·
temporary tournament · safari · glow · winter.

Each layout can differ in: tee pads · basket positions · pars · hole distances · hole order · OB rules ·
mandatory routes · course notes · difficulty ratings. Temporary tournament layouts appear and disappear
automatically based on event dates.

## 5. Digital Scorecards
Unlimited scorecards for: solo · group · singles · doubles · triples · larger teams · casual ·
practice · league · tournament · custom courses · custom layouts.

Players can: select course and layout · choose starting holes · add registered players · add guests ·
add previous cardmates · add nearby players · create teams · choose a scoring format · record scores
hole by hole · record penalty strokes · adjust a hole's par · skip holes · play holes out of order ·
add private round notes · add notes for specific holes · edit scores during or after the round · enter
an old round manually · delete a scorecard · exclude a practice round from official statistics · share
a completed scorecard · **save a round without keeping score**.

The scorecard can show: hole score · score relative to par · current total · player position · birdies ·
pars · bogeys · double bogeys · front-nine total · back-nine total · final result · tee order ·
estimated finish time.

## 6. Shared Group Scoring
Everyone on a card accesses the same scorecard: automatic sharing · live score synchronization ·
multiple players entering scores · score verification · **score-editing history** · guest-player
support · nearby-player detection · saved cardmates · team creation · automatic tee-order calculation ·
audio tee-order announcements. One person can keep score for the group, or each player enters their own.

## 7. Basic and Advanced Scoring Modes
**Basic** — enter only total throws per hole.
**Advanced statistical** — record where each throw finished: fairway · off the fairway · circle 2 ·
circle 1 · parked · out-of-bounds · penalty area · in the basket.
**Map-based** — place each throw's landing position on the map; the app draws the path through the hole.

## 8. Throw Tracking
Each throw can include: starting location · landing location · throw distance · disc used · throw type ·
backhand or forehand · drive/approach/putt · landing zone · OB result · penalty · personal note.

After the round: every throw on the map · total distance thrown · longest throw · average throw
distance · disc used on each shot · missed fairways · putting locations · OB locations · where strokes
were gained or lost.

## 9. GPS Rangefinder
Show: current distance to basket · to a selected landing area · to a hazard · to a dogleg · remaining
distance after each throw · to front or back of a landing zone. Can appear inside the scorecard, on the
hole map, on the phone's lock screen, or on a connected smartwatch.

## 10. Measure a Throw
Mark the throwing location, walk to the disc, mark the landing location. Record: measured distance ·
disc used · date · location · wind · throw type · direction · notes. Compare: longest throws · average
distance by disc · forehand vs backhand · distance changes over time · plastics or weights ·
practice-field performance.

## 11. Player Statistics
Automatic: total rounds · total holes · average round score · average vs par · personal bests · aces ·
birdies · pars · bogeys · double bogeys · birdie % · par % · bogey % · bogey-free rounds · average by
course · average by layout · best score on each hole · average on each hole · longest birdie streak ·
longest par-or-better streak.

Advanced: C1 putting % · C2 putting % · fairway-hit % · greens in regulation · scramble % · parked % ·
OB % · penalty rate · average putting distance · driving, approach and putting performance.

Filterable by: latest rounds · current month · current year · previous year · all time · specific
course · specific layout · casual · league · tournament.

## 12. Round and Player Ratings
Per-round rating from: final score · course difficulty · layout difficulty · expected score ·
historical results on that layout. Plus an overall rating from recent rated rounds, so players compare
performances across courses without relying on raw scores. Show: current rating · history · highest ·
average round rating · recent changes · best-rated rounds · trends.

## 13. Hole Comparisons
Per hole: global average · local average · player's average · player's best · birdie % · par % ·
bogey % · average by skill level · difficulty ranking · common landing areas · common OB areas.
Helps players see where they regularly gain or lose strokes.

## 14. Round Activity Tracking
GPS walking route · steps · distance walked · duration · average pace · time per hole · estimated
calories · start time · finish time · weather. Afterwards, a map of their movement through the course.

## 15. Disc Collection and Bag Management
Each disc: manufacturer · mold · plastic · weight · color · flight numbers · stability · type ·
purchase date · purchase price · notes · photograph · lost/found status.

Players can: build multiple bags · sort by type or color · mark active or stored · track measured
throws per disc · view average and longest distance · record which discs were used in a round ·
compare similar discs · mark a disc lost · record where it was last used · add contact info for
returning lost discs.

## 16. Practice Tools
Modes: putting · accuracy · approach · driving · distance · field work · personal challenges.
Putting tracks: distance · attempts · makes · misses · % made · streaks · personal records · C1 and C2
performance. Accuracy tracks: distance · target area · successful throws · miss direction · disc used ·
forehand or backhand · improvement over time.

## 17. Friends and Community
Add friends · follow players · find previous cardmates · view public profiles · compare statistics ·
view recent rounds · send round invitations · create private groups · join local clubs · share
scorecards · post accomplishments · comment on rounds · react to achievements · find players nearby ·
create recurring playing groups. **Privacy controls** over who sees rounds, location, statistics and
profile.

## 18. Achievements and Rewards
Achievements for: first round · first birdie · first ace · first bogey-free round · personal best ·
new course · multiple states · completing a league · winning an event · putting goals · consecutive
days · round milestones · difficult weather · all courses in an area.
Rewards: badges · profile icons · player titles · course trophies · streak counters · unlockable
themes · animated celebrations · fantasy rewards.

## 19. Smartwatch Scoring
View current hole · par and distance · enter scores · move between holes · view totals · remaining
distance · tee-order notifications · basic statistics. The phone stays in a pocket for most of the round.

## 20. Offline Play
Works without cell service: keeping score · preloaded course map · saved scorecards · player
statistics · saved discs and players · throw tracking · throw measurement · putting practice · accuracy
practice · GPS activity. Syncs automatically on reconnect.

## 21. League Features
Find and join nearby leagues. Schedules · recurring weekly events · registration · check-in ·
divisions · singles · doubles · larger teams · handicap scoring · flex starts · shotgun starts · tee
times · live scoring · live leaderboards · season standings · player statistics · announcements · push
and email notifications · entry-fee tracking · payout tracking · bag-tag tracking · closest-to-the-pin ·
ace pools.

## 22. Tournament and Event Features
TDs can: create public or private events · set registration limits · accept registrations · create
divisions · check in players · assign tee times · assign starting holes · build and randomize cards ·
create teams · create multiple pools · use multiple courses and layouts · run multi-round events ·
publish rules and schedules · add hole-specific OB instructions · monitor live scores · correct scores ·
add penalties · mark DNF · verify scorecards · break ties · record payouts · publish results.
Formats: shotgun start · tee times · flex start.

## 23. Live Leaderboards
Position · player name · division · score relative to par · holes completed · current hole · round
score · total event score · movement up or down · projected finish · card status. For players,
organizers and spectators in real time.

## 24. Course Traffic
Current active groups · estimated wait at the first tee · typical traffic by day and time · scheduled
leagues and events · peak hours · estimated course-clearing time. Estimated from active scorecards and
historical activity.

## 25. Course Reviews and Photos
Rate a course · write reviews · rate individual categories · upload photographs · report conditions ·
mark reviews helpful · sort by newest/most helpful/recommended · suggest corrections · report
inappropriate content.
Categories: course design · maintenance · navigation · scenery · tee pads · baskets · signage · safety ·
beginner friendliness · overall experience.

## 26. Course-Management Tools
Approved managers can: add and move tees · add and move baskets · draw fairways · add doglegs · add OB
areas · add mandatory routes · add drop zones · create layouts · update basket positions · change pars
and distances · add hole notes · upload tee-sign photos · update hours and fees · post closures ·
update amenities · add temporary tournament holes · retire outdated layouts · review suggested edits ·
publish announcements. **When a basket moves, connected layouts and distances update automatically.**

## 27. Course Analytics
Total recorded rounds · unique players · estimated recreation hours · average daily traffic · busiest
days · busiest times · visitor locations · tourism information · layout usage · rating history ·
hole-by-hole scoring · average round duration · repeat-player rate. Downloadable for cities, parks
departments, sponsors, clubs, property owners and funding organizations.

## 28. Store Directory
Store name · location · hours · contact · website · photographs · products offered · course proximity ·
customer reviews · directions. Stores can advertise sales, events, demo days and new products.

## 29. Fantasy Disc Golf
Create fantasy leagues · invite friends · draft professional players · set draft order · snake, auction
or round-robin drafts · make picks for individual tournaments · set weekly lineups · track professional
results · earn points from real tournament performance · complete trades · use waivers · view standings ·
head-to-head matchups · join public contests · follow live projected points.

Scoring can include: final placement · round score · birdies · bogey-free rounds · aces · hot rounds ·
lead-card appearances · strokes gained · tournament wins · missed-cut penalties.

## 30. Animated Live Course Viewer
Animated players progressing through each hole: avatars · tee assignments · players gathering at the
hole · throwing animations · flying discs · walking animations · putting animations · basket reactions ·
score popups · live position changes · fantasy-point notifications · birdie and ace celebrations.

**The animation must reflect the actual status of the card. Players should NOT restart their animations
whenever the screen is reopened** — it must use saved round progress and timing to determine whether
players are waiting at the tee, throwing drives, walking the fairway, throwing approaches, putting,
completing the hole, or moving to the next tee. *(The owner has raised this repeatedly.)*

## 31. Notifications
Upcoming tee times · league registration · event check-in · published cards · starting-hole assignments ·
score verification · friend invitations · fantasy lineup deadlines · player withdrawals · tournament
starting times · lead changes · personal records · course closures · basket-position changes · severe
weather · nearby events. **Players control each category.**

## 32. Free and Premium Features
**Free** — unlimited basic scorecards · course directory · course maps · group scoring · basic
statistics · recent round history · throw measurement · practice tools · league participation · event
participation · offline scoring · basic fantasy leagues.

**Premium** — complete lifetime statistics · advanced performance analysis · round and player ratings ·
hole-by-hole comparisons · GPS lock-screen rangefinder · real-time course traffic · smartwatch scoring ·
advanced leaderboards · unlimited fantasy leagues · advanced fantasy analytics · custom profile themes ·
additional animated avatars · ad-free · downloadable reports.

**The goal is to make the free version completely usable**, and charge for deeper analysis,
personalization, convenience and premium entertainment.
