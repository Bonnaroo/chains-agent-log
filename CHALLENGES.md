# CHALLENGES.md — how trophies, challenges, home course and crowns fit together

The promise: **every round moves something forward.** A better average, a hole finally solved, a collection
advanced, a title held, a crew goal fed. Playing is the reward; the app adds evidence and recognition.
No gimmicks (no "putt from one foot"); everything here is real disc golf that also makes you better.

Sources we lean on (principles, not mechanics): the STEP UP trial (competition + support raise activity),
walking-competition data (mismatched opponents weaken it → match by ability / reward improvement), Mekler et
al. (points/badges raise output, not intrinsic motivation → badges alone aren't the product), Strava's split
of "fastest" vs "Local Legend" (dedication deserves its own recognition), WoW dailies/weeklies/achievements/
titles, Audible's tiered achievements, Duolingo's streak freeze.

## 1. Four things a player gets
Progress ("I'm getting better") · Purpose ("something worth trying next round") · Identity ("this is my course,
these are my accomplishments") · Connection ("my friends and I have something going").

## 2. Three time scales, one screen (Challenges)
- **This week** — 3 required (scaled to the player's last four weeks) + 2 bonus "go after it". Resets Monday.
  All three = *perfect week*; perfect weeks → streak; one free miss a month (streak protection).
- **This season** — monthly/seasonal set (e.g. "November: 10 rounds · 30 birdies · 3 courses") with one
  reward only available in that window.
- **Trophies** — the permanent ladders (88 now; target ~100), each with progress to the next tier, points per
  tier, a chosen *title* under your name, and 3 pinned badges on the profile.

## 3. Three tracks (so beginners and grinders both have something)
| Track | Compared to | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Personal | your own established baseline | beat your ghost, raise the floor, new normal, problem solved (hole) |
| Peer | players of similar ability (division / handicap) | division challenge, handicap cup |
| Open | everyone at the course | course record, birdie leader, local regular |

Baseline rules: built from several eligible rounds, frozen for the duration of a challenge, same layout where
possible, big improvement awards need repeated success, new players are *provisional*, and a deliberately bad
baseline round can't be rewarded (floor = your median, not your worst).

## 4. Home course (the anchor)
Player sets one (default: most played). Its block shows record, average, the hole that beats you, holes birdied
lifetime (the **birdie collection**), and the next course-specific challenge.
**Mastery map** — every hole has your history and a next target:
- Breakthrough: first result better than your baseline on that hole
- Repeatable: do it again on another round
- Reliable: hit the target on 3 of your next 5 visits
- New best: your lowest ever on the hole
- Birdie collected: first birdie (fills the collection map; front nine / back nine / full card)
The weekly "hole" challenge is always one of your three hardest holes at home.

## 5. Crowns (held titles at a course — several, so grinders and sharpshooters both get one)
| Crown | Earned by | Fairness rule |
|---|---|---|
| Course record | best full round (all time) | ties → earlier |
| Course champion | best full round this season | resets each season |
| Local regular | most distinct days played, rolling 90 days | days, not rounds |
| Birdie leader | most *different* holes birdied this season | — |
| Birdie total / Birdie rate (per hole) | most birdies on hole N / best birdie % on hole N | rate needs ≥10 attempts |
| Hole specialist (owner) | best average on hole N, last 5 plays, 90 days | needs ≥3 plays (shipped) |
| Most improved | biggest sustained drop in 5-round average vs baseline | needs 10+ rounds |
| Steady | best average, 3+ rounds | — |
Losing a crown keeps the dated achievement ("Held course record, Oct–Nov 2026"). Titles show while held.
Permanent **firsts** (first round here, first birdie on hole N, first ace…) stay as they are.

## 6. Crew (later, once there are local players)
Crew birdie map (together birdie every hole in a month), team improvement (everyone meets a personal target),
home-and-away, digital bag tags, season cup with limited counting rounds. Cap contributions per person so one
player with unlimited time can't decide everything. Crews never override an individual's names.

## 7. Challenge catalogue (pool to draw from)
**Personal improvement:** beat your ghost · raise the floor · new normal · break the barrier · back it up ·
fewer big numbers · stronger finish · opening statement · reliable golf.
**Hole & course mastery:** first bird (hole) · unfinished business · problem solved · front nine collected ·
back nine collected · complete collection · clean stretch · bogey-free nine · bogey-free round · course graduate.
**Scoring:** first under par · personal best · turkey · birdie run (5) · bounce back · recovery round · first
eagle · ace (witnessed recognition separate) · closing time.
**Practice (opt-in tracking only):** putting baseline/progress · circle confidence · range builder · landing
zone · fairway finder · penalty reduction · practice pays (describe both improvements; never claim causation).
**Exploration/participation:** course explorer (5/10/25/50) · local circuit · layout explorer · return visit ·
nine counts · season regular · monthly rhythm · road trip · league debut · season finisher.
**Friends/crew:** rivalry series (best of 5) · handicap cup · doubles partnership · crew birdie map · team
improvement · home-and-away · bag tags · season cup · shared target · rematch.
**Fantasy (separate from playing skill):** weekly winner · season champion · podium sweep · value pick · deep
field · comeback manager · major specialist · rivalry winner.

## 8. Rewards and moments
| Accomplishment | What happens |
|---|---|
| small milestone | one quiet line in the recap |
| personal breakthrough | permanent badge + round highlight |
| course mastery | course trophy with its signature hole |
| seasonal win | dated title + trophy |
| crown held | title shown while held; dated achievement when lost |
| lifetime milestone | profile treatment + shareable career card |
Every badge says exactly what happened ("Problem solved — hole 12: bogey or better on 3 of 5 rounds; you
averaged 5.8 before"). Reveals: weekly = a check; perfect week = a card; trophy tier = the full reveal. Sound
and haptics are optional polish, never before the next throw, always mutable. Monthly recap card on the 1st.

## 9. Trust
Personal records: self-reported. Friend competitions: shared card + participant confirmation. Public crowns:
minimum rounds, verified rounds (another Chains player on the card), a way to dispute. Practice, mulligans,
doubles and competitive singles are always labelled. GPS supports presence, never proves a score.

## 10. Measure in our own app
First-round completion · second round within 30 days · playing days per active player · challenge
acceptance/completion · beginner vs experienced return rate · "did this make the round more fun?" · scoring
time. Not app opens.
